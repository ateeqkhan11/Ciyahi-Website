Every automation project I've seen starts the same way: a pile of unstructured documents lands in an inbox, and nothing downstream can run until someone answers one question —

**"What document is this, and what does it mean?"**

We recently built the answer for an insurance brokerage.

Every workflow there — new business submissions, renewals, claims, audits — begins as an unsorted mix of ACORD forms, loss runs, SOVs, COIs, and correspondence. PDFs, scans, email threads, even fax.

So before automating anything, we built the foundation:

📄 A taxonomy of **60+ document types** across 8 categories
🔍 An LLM classification pipeline that knows the difference between "an ACORD form" and "an ACORD 125, Commercial Application"
✅ Type-aware extraction, completeness validation, and cataloging with full audit trail

Cataloging was only step one. On top of that data layer, we built complete automated workflows — underwriting triage, renewal prep, compliance evidence, proposal generation.

Here's the part that matters beyond insurance: **the pattern is industry-agnostic**. Classify → Extract → Validate → Catalog. Swap the taxonomy, keep the pipeline. Hospitals, legal, logistics, lending — if your industry runs on documents, this applies.

It's the same spine that drives InvoAIce, our AP automation product, live in production today.

Classify once. Automate everywhere.
